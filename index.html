How it works

Three steps, and you only do the first one.

01

Build your list

Type it, paste it from your notes app, or add things as you think of them. Deal Basket matches each line to a real product, store brands included.

02

We price it

Every item resolves to an actual shelf price, not an estimate — line-by-line costs and a running total. This is the part that has to be right before we go live, and it's what we're working on now.

03

Shop knowing the number

Take the list in with the total already in hand, and check items off as you go.

What's in the app

Built for the shop you already do every week.

Prices

Real shelf prices, not estimates

Questions

The short answers.

Is Deal Basket free?

Yes — all of it, with no card to enter. There are no paid plans on sale today; see pricing. Worth saying plainly: we're not live yet, so the app has no store prices in it right now. Lists, pricing and alerts arrive when we go live.

Which stores are covered?

None, today. We ran on a single chain in the Longview, Texas area and have paused while we work on wider coverage. We would rather say that than imply a coverage map we do not have. Send us your ZIP and we'll tell you when we cover your stores.

How current are the prices?

There are no prices in the app right now — we're not live yet. When we launch, every basket carries the timestamp of the data behind it, so you always know how fresh the number is.

Do you sell my shopping data?

No. We don't sell or rent personal information. What we collect and why is written out in plain language in the Privacy Policy.

Are you affiliated with the stores you list?

No. Deal Basket is independent. We're not sponsored by any retailer, and store rankings are based on price, not on who pays us.

Get in before launch.

We're building coverage region by region. Send us your ZIP code and we'll tell you when Deal Basket covers your stores — and get you into the beta when it opens.
