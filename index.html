How it works

Three steps, and you only do the first one.

01

Build your list

Type it, paste it from your notes app, or add things as you think of them. Deal Basket matches each line to a real product, store brands included.

02

We price it

Every item resolves to an actual shelf price, refreshed each night. You get line-by-line costs and a running total, not an estimate.

03

Shop knowing the number

Take the list in with the total already in hand, and check items off as you go.

Questions

The short answers.

Is Deal Basket free?

Yes — all of it, with no card to enter. Real shelf prices refreshed every night, your list priced before you shop, price alerts on the items you watch, and a monthly view of where your grocery money went. There are no paid plans on sale today; see pricing.

Which stores are covered?

Right now: one chain, Kroger, in the Longview, Texas area. That is genuinely all — we would rather say so than imply a coverage map we do not have. We add stores as we verify their pricing data, and enter your ZIP when you sign up and we'll tell you what's live near you.

How current are the prices?

Prices refresh every night. We show you the timestamp on every basket so you always know how fresh the number is.

Do you sell my shopping data?

No. We don't sell or rent personal information. What we collect and why is written out in plain language in the Privacy Policy.

Are you affiliated with the stores you list?

No. Deal Basket is independent. We're not sponsored by any retailer, and store rankings are based on price, not on who pays us.

Get in before launch.

We're onboarding early testers by region. Send us your ZIP code and we'll tell you when Deal Basket covers your stores — and get you a TestFlight invite when it does.
