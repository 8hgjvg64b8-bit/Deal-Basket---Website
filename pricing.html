Billing questions

How paying works.

Do I have to pay to use Deal Basket?

No. The Basic plan is free permanently and includes the full price comparison across every store we cover, including the smart basket split. Most people never need to upgrade.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, so you cancel in your device settings, not in our app. On iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling before the trial ends means you're never charged.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, at any time, in the same subscription settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and the app stores prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Your lists and savings history stay in your account — features above your plan level simply stop being available until you upgrade again.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds for App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies, since they process the payment. If something went wrong, email support@dealbasket.app and we'll help you sort it out with them.

Will the price change later?

If we ever change the price of a plan you're on, we'll tell you by email before it takes effect, and you'll have the chance to cancel first. We won't quietly start charging more.
