Billing questions

How paying works.

Do I have to pay to use Deal Basket?

No. Deal Basket is free, and today that is the whole product: real shelf prices refreshed every night, your list priced before you shop, price alerts on the items you watch, and a monthly view of where your grocery money went.

How do I cancel?

Open your account settings in the app or on the website and choose Manage subscription — that opens a secure billing portal where you can cancel in one click. You can also email support@dealbasket.app and we'll do it for you.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, at any time from your billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference so you only pay for what you use. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Your lists and savings history stay in your account — features above your plan level simply stop being available until you upgrade again.

Do you offer refunds?

If something went wrong or you were charged unexpectedly, email support@dealbasket.app within 14 days and we'll sort it out.

Will the price change later?

If we ever change the price of a plan you're on, we'll tell you by email before it takes effect, and you'll have the chance to cancel first. We won't quietly start charging more.
